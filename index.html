About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate (B.Sc. Hons.) currently completing an MCA, with just over two years of hands-on experience across full stack development, technical support and teaching. On the software side that means building and maintaining web applications and RESTful APIs with Node.js, Express.js, React.js and MySQL/MongoDB — debugging live sites, fixing bugs and shipping features for real freelance clients.

Alongside that, I train students in robotics, AI, IoT and 3D printing — working with Arduino, sensors, actuators and embedded systems, and helping learners go from an idea to a working prototype. A summer research internship at IIT Roorkee's Department of Electrical Engineering, building a Power Electronic Buck Converter with an Arduino controller, sits right at the intersection of both sides of what I do: software and hardware.
